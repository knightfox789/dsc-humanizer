# Examples

## Donor report

### Before
The project has played a pivotal role in empowering village communities by fostering sustainable and inclusive water management practices.

### After
Village institutions are increasingly using water-budget findings to discuss local water demand, available supply, and priority actions for the Water Security Plan.

---

## Technical

### Before
A comprehensive assessment was undertaken to understand the overall groundwater scenario of the intervention villages.

### After
The team assessed groundwater conditions using open wells and borewells, including static water level, pumping water level, seasonal fluctuation, and historical trends.

---

## Case study

### Before
The intervention brought a remarkable transformation in the life of the farmer.

### After
After adopting the demonstrated practice, the farmer reduced input use and reported a measurable improvement in crop performance.

Only use the second sentence when the source actually supports both changes.
