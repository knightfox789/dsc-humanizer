# Technical Writing Style

## Rules

- Keep established technical terms.
- State units consistently.
- Separate methodology from interpretation.
- Do not hide uncertainty.
- Keep formulas and indicator definitions stable.
- Avoid replacing technical precision with generic development-sector language.
- Prefer short explanatory sentences around complex data.

## Preferred pattern

**Method → Result → Interpretation**

Example:
> Seasonal crop-water demand was estimated from crop area and irrigation depth. Demand was aggregated across Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons. The result was then compared with available water supply to estimate the annual deficit or surplus.
