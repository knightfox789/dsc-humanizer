# AI Patterns to Reduce

Reduce these when they appear mechanically or repeatedly:

## Inflated significance
- plays a pivotal role
- underscores the importance
- stands as a testament
- marks a significant milestone
- transformative journey

## Generic transitions
- furthermore
- moreover
- additionally
- in conclusion
- it is important to note

Use them only when they genuinely help.

## Repetitive structures
- every paragraph beginning with a topic sentence followed by exactly three supporting points;
- repeated "not only ... but also";
- repeated triads;
- identical sentence lengths;
- excessive em dashes;
- excessive colon-led lists.

## Vague development language
- empowering communities
- fostering resilience
- driving sustainable change
- enhancing livelihoods
- strengthening ecosystems

Replace vague claims with specific actions and evidence when the source provides them.
