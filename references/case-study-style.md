# Case Study Style

## Structure

1. Context
2. Constraint
3. Intervention
4. Local action
5. Result
6. Learning

## Voice

Human-centered does not mean fictionalized.

Use:
- observed detail;
- farmer or community actions;
- concrete changes;
- supported figures.

Do not invent:
- quotes;
- emotions;
- family background;
- conversations;
- motivations;
- causal explanations not in the source.
