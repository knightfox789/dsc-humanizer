# Donor Report Style

## Tone

Use:
- restrained confidence;
- evidence-led language;
- clear distinction between activity, output, outcome, and impact;
- direct description of what changed;
- numbers when supported;
- practical interpretation.

Avoid:
- promotional adjectives;
- "transformative";
- "groundbreaking";
- "remarkable";
- "pivotal";
- "testament";
- "revolutionary";
- unsupported "empowerment";
- unsupported "resilience";
- unsupported "sustainability".

## Preferred structure

1. What was done
2. Who participated
3. Where / scale
4. What changed
5. Evidence
6. Remaining issue or next step

## Example

Weak:
> The initiative successfully empowered communities to embrace sustainable water management.

Better:
> Village institutions used the water budget to review local demand and supply conditions and identify priority interventions for the annual Water Security Plan.
