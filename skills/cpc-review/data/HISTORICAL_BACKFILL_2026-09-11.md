## Data-quality rule

`benchmark_reference_only=Y` on recovered quote rows means the rate may be used as supplementary historical context but is not an approved award benchmark. `master_item_history.csv` remains limited to finalized awards.

If later PO, invoice, payment, purchase-completion or final CPC approval records are recovered, these cases should be re-reviewed and upgraded only where the evidence supports it.
